Our Programs

We offer a variety of programs to suit the needs of our students. Our programs are designed to provide quality education and practical skills that prepare students for the job market.

Higher National Diploma (HND)

The Higher National Diploma is a national higher education diploma issued by the Ministry of Higher Education.
HND is a two-year (three-year for Health Sciences) professional university program focusing on practical skills and knowledge, equivalent to the first two years of a university degree.

Requirements for HND Exams

To sit for the HND exam, you'll need:

  • A complete program of study in an approved institution (Heriatge Higher Institute of Peace and Development Studies)
  • A high school leaving certificate (e.g.,GCE Advanced Level, Baccalaureate)
  • Transcripts and Internship reports

DEGREE (B-Tech, BA)

Our degree program is a one-year program after the HND. It is also referred to as a top-up degree program.
We offer these programs through our National Affiliated University partners (University of Bamenda, University of Maroua)
